# Internet banking slow login after DNS change

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-0905
**Date:** 2025-09-05
**Severity:** SEV3
**Affected service:** internet-banking
**Duration:** 40 minutes

## Customer impact
Around 3,000 customers saw slow logins (8-10 seconds). No failed payments.

## Timeline
- 09:10 DNS TTL change deployed
- 09:20 Login latency alerts
- 09:50 DNS change rolled back

## Root cause
A DNS TTL change pointed half of the traffic to a cold standby load balancer.

**Root cause category:** configuration drift

## Corrective actions
- Add DNS changes to the change advisory board checklist.
