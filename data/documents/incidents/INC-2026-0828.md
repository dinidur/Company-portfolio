# Batch reconciliation job exhausted payment DB connections

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0828
**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Severity:** SEV2
**Affected service:** card-payment-gateway
**Duration:** 25 minutes

## Customer impact
Card payments slowed and 4% failed during the morning.

## Timeline
- 09:00 Reconciliation batch started late
- 09:10 Errors
- 09:35 Batch paused

## Root cause
The monthly reconciliation batch ran in business hours and opened 60 connections on the same pool as the online payment gateway.

**Root cause category:** database connection pool exhaustion

## Corrective actions
- Separate connection pool for batch jobs.
- Batch jobs only in the 00:00-05:00 window.
