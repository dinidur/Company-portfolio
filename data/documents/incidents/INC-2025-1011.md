# Card payment gateway timeouts during Deepavali promotion

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-1011
**Date:** 2025-10-11
**Severity:** SEV1
**Affected service:** card-payment-gateway
**Duration:** 1 hour 25 minutes

## Customer impact
About 18% of card payments failed with timeout between 19:05 and 20:30. Estimated 41,000 failed transactions. Merchants complained on social media.

## Timeline
- 19:05 Error rate alert on card-payment-gateway
- 19:15 On-call sees DB wait time spike
- 19:40 Pool size increased manually from 50 to 120
- 20:30 Error rate back to normal

## Root cause
The payments database connection pool (HikariCP, max 50) was exhausted. Promotion traffic was 3x normal and slow fraud-check queries held connections for up to 9 seconds. New requests waited for a connection and timed out at the gateway.

**Root cause category:** database connection pool exhaustion

## Corrective actions
- Load test before every promotion campaign.
- Add an alert on pool usage above 80%.
- Move fraud-check reads to a read replica.
