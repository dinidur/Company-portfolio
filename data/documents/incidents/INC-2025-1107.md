# IslandPay switch timeouts for interbank transfers

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-1107
**Date:** 2025-11-07
**Severity:** SEV2
**Affected service:** interbank-transfer
**Duration:** 55 minutes

## Customer impact
Interbank instant transfers (NovaPay Instant) failed or stayed pending. 9,200 transfers affected, all auto-reversed within 24 hours.

## Timeline
- 10:02 Spike in pending transfers
- 10:20 IslandPay confirms latency on their side
- 10:57 IslandPay recovers

## Root cause
The third-party IslandPay national switch had high latency (p99 > 25s). Our transfer service has no circuit breaker, so threads were blocked waiting for the switch and the queue backed up.

**Root cause category:** third-party switch timeout

## Corrective actions
- Implement a circuit breaker with a 5s timeout for IslandPay calls.
- Show a clear 'pending' message in the mobile app instead of an error.
