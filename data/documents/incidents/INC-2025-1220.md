# Expired TLS certificate on card acquiring endpoint

**Incident ID:** INC-2025-1220
**Date:** 2025-12-20
**Severity:** SEV1
**Affected service:** card-acquiring
**Duration:** 2 hours 10 minutes

## Customer impact
POS terminals at merchants could not connect. All card-present transactions at our merchants failed during the Christmas shopping peak.

## Timeline
- 00:00 Certificate expired
- 08:30 Merchant calls start
- 09:40 Root cause found
- 10:40 New certificate installed

## Root cause
The TLS certificate for acquiring.novabank.example expired. Renewal was a manual task owned by one engineer who was on leave. No expiry monitoring existed.

**Root cause category:** certificate expiry

## Corrective actions
- Automate certificate renewal (ACME / internal PKI).
- Alert 30, 14 and 3 days before expiry.
