# IslandPay switch timeouts - Sinhala and Tamil New Year peak

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0419
**Date:** 2026-04-19
**Severity:** SEV2
**Affected service:** interbank-transfer
**Duration:** 1 hour 5 minutes

## Customer impact
Around 15,000 instant transfers stayed pending during the New Year shopping peak.

## Timeline
- 18:00 Pending transfers increase
- 18:40 IslandPay confirms congestion
- 19:05 Recovered

## Root cause
Same pattern as INC-2025-1107. The circuit breaker was still in the backlog and not deployed, so transfer workers were blocked by the slow switch.

**Root cause category:** third-party switch timeout

## Corrective actions
- Circuit breaker moved to the top of the Q2 backlog (owner: Payments Platform team).
- Queue-based async transfer submission.
