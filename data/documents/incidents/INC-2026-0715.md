# Intermediate CA certificate expired on payment API

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0715
**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Severity:** SEV2
**Affected service:** payments-api
**Duration:** 45 minutes

## Customer impact
Partner fintechs using our payments API got TLS errors.

## Timeline
- 06:00 Partner errors
- 06:45 Chain updated

## Root cause
The leaf certificate was renewed automatically, but the intermediate CA certificate in the chain was not tracked by the new automation.

**Root cause category:** certificate expiry

## Corrective actions
- Track full chain expiry, not only leaf certificates.
