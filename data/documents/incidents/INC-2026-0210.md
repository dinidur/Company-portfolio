# HR portal login outage

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0210
**Date:** 2026-02-10
**Severity:** SEV3
**Affected service:** hr-portal
**Duration:** 2 hours

## Customer impact
Staff could not apply for leave. No customer impact.

## Timeline
- 08:00 SSO errors
- 10:00 Fixed

## Root cause
SAML signing certificate of the HR portal was rotated on the IdP but not on the portal.

**Root cause category:** certificate expiry

## Corrective actions
- Add the HR portal to the certificate inventory.
